Technical XR Analyst Forensic Breakdown

  • Contextual Affordance Activation: Detecting eligible inventory items ("Paper Currency / Cash"), the game's UI system dynamically spawns an unlit billboard prompt showing a banknote glyph, currency symbol ($), quantity requirement (x3), and a downward-pointing guidance chevron directed into the pot.

The interaction registers within a single discrete frame (<33.3ms), causing immediate item subtraction from the player's inventory buffer and instantaneous retirement of the UI affordance.

Positional Translation, Scaling Logic, Velocity Handoff & Visual Effects

  • World-Space Billboarding: The UI widget is positioned at fixed world coordinates relative to the cooking pot center: (Δx: 0.0m, Δy: +0.35m, Δz: 0.0m). A constrained yaw-billboard transformation matrix continuously re-orients the quad normal vector toward the observer's HMD camera position (LookAt(HMD.position, Vector3.up)), eliminating perspective shearing during rapid head rotation.
  • Harmonic Micro-Floating Oscillation: The prompt features a procedural vertical floating animation (sine-wave oscillation: ~1.2 Hz frequency, ~15mm amplitude), giving the affordance a lifelike hovering presence distinct from the surrounding static physical geometry.
  • Luminance & Shader Behavior: Rendered through an Unlit / Self-Emissive Alpha-Blended shader pass. The graphic maintains 100% flat white luminance (#FFFFFF) completely impervious to dynamic point light attenuation or ambient night shadows, preventing visibility collapse against roaring 2000K flame meshes.
  • Zero-Latency Dismissal vs. Physical Explosion Velocity Handoff:
    • The UI billboard component undergoes a zero-latency step dismissal (alpha 1.0 → 0.0 with 0ms transition duration) without particle residue.
    • At Frame 0116–0124, the game executes a dramatic physicalized velocity handoff: the campfire structure fractures dynamically. Over 15 independent log splinters and charred stone fragments are instantiated as rigidbodies with high radial linear velocities (v_radial ≈ 3.5 - 6.0 m/s) and angular tumble (ω ≈ 180 - 720 deg/s) erupting outward across the viewport.

Destination State of the Spatial UI Component

  • State Machine: DORMANT_COLLIDER ➔ ACTIVE_AFFORDANCE ➔ TRIGGER_COMMIT ➔ DESTROYED.
  • Destination State: DESTROYED (Despawned from Scene Graph).

Because the action represents an irreversible crafting/ignition commitment (3x Cash consumed into fuel/recipe), the UI component's quad mesh and collider are destroyed immediately to free up the spatial interaction channel.

Ergonomic & Spatial UI/UX Evaluation for VR/XR

  • Vergence-Accommodation Conflict Mitigation: Anchoring the recipe billboard directly in world-space at the cooking pot depth (~0.85m to 1.2m from the player's eyes) eliminates stereoscopic focal disparity, avoiding eye fatigue that screen-locked HUD overlays induce.
  • Motion Sickness Prevention: Billboarding only on the vertical Yaw axis prevents confusing roll/pitch tilt in the user's peripheral field during head tilts.
