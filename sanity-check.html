💡 The Rationale Why: Why Systematic Sanity Checks Are Non-Negotiable

In high-fidelity VR research, faulty video decoders, missing image frames, or silent 404 network errors invalidate temporal analysis. A missing frame during a 33.3ms controller trigger actuation could falsely suggest an instant state change rather than a physical transition. This sanity check suite exercises the live environment, testing file headers, network availability, and browser rendering engines before results are presented to clients or stakeholders.
